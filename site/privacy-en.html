Privacy Policy – Tappa: Prossima Area

Last updated: 18 October 2026

Tappa: Prossima Area (“the app”) shows the next service area along the Italian motorway, with distances, fuel prices, EV charging points, brands and facilities, and lets you plan a route. This page explains what data the app uses and what happens to it.

In short: the owner does not collect, store or sell any personal data. There are no accounts, no advertising, no usage statistics and no tracking.

Owner and contact

The owner is Carlo Santacroce. For any question write to iviaggi.autostradeitaliane@gmail.com.

Device location

The app uses your location only to work out which motorway you are on, which direction you are travelling in and how far away the next service areas are. It asks for the “While Using the App” permission. If you connect your iPhone to CarPlay, the location may also be used while the app is in the background, to update the list shown in the car.

The calculation takes place entirely on your device. Your location is not sent to the owner or to any server of the app, and it is not stored.

Route planner

When you plan a route, the names of the starting point, destination and stops you type are sent to Apple, which converts them into coordinates and calculates the route through the Maps service. This processing is carried out by Apple under its own privacy policy. The owner does not receive this data.

The toll and fuel cost shown are estimates calculated on the device, based on the motorway operators’ rates and your Settings.

Your settings

Vehicle type, fuel, average consumption, currency, distance unit and similar settings are saved only on your device and are used to calculate the estimates. They are not sent anywhere else. If you delete the app, they are erased.

CarPlay

With CarPlay the app shows the list of the next service areas on the car screen and, when you tap one, its prices and facilities. The app does not send any other personal data to the car and does not receive any from it.

Data sources and limits

Fuel prices and stations come from the open data of the Italian Ministry of Enterprises and Made in Italy (Osservaprezzi Carburanti), published under the IODL 2.0 licence.

Charging point data comes from the national charging infrastructure registry (PUN, MASE / GSE, via onData, CC BY 4.0 licence), from the official websites of motorway operators and charging operators (including Autostrada del Brennero) and from the operators’ apps. It may be incomplete or out of date; each card shows when the data was last checked.

Information on service areas, brands and facilities is collected and checked by the owner and may be incomplete or out of date. Prices, tolls and availability should always be checked on site. The app is independent and is not affiliated with any motorway operator or public body. Brands and logos belong to their respective owners.

Data updates

When opened, the app may download from a server a file with updated data (service areas, prices, charging points). It does not send personal data or your location; as with any internet connection, whoever runs the server can see the IP address. If the server does not respond, the app uses the data already saved on your phone.

Safety while driving

The app must not distract you from driving. Use it only when it is safe and always obey the rules of the road.

Cookies, advertising, third parties

The app uses no cookies, analytics tools, advertising or third-party libraries that collect data.

Your rights

Because the owner does not collect personal data, there is no data to access, correct or delete. You can still ask for information by writing to the address above, and you may contact the Italian data protection authority (garanteprivacy.it) or the authority of your own country.

Children

The app does not ask for or collect personal data from anyone, and therefore not from children.

Changes

If the app later adds features that process data (for example reviews or price photos sent by users), this page will be updated before release and the date above will change.
